# Adds option to retrieve CVC tokens on Confirmation Tokens

## What’s new

Lets you specify the `cvc_update` token collected from the [Payment Element](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/payment-element.md) for a [Confirmation Token](https://docs.stripe.com/api/confirmation_tokens.md?api-version=2024-09-30.acacia).

## Impact

This change lets you use CVC numbers with Confirmation Tokens.
