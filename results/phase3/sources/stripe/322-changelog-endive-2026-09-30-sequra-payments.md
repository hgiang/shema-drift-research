# Adds support for SeQura payments

## What’s new

Adds support for [SeQura](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/sequra.md), a buy now, pay later (BNPL) payment method available in Spain. You can use SeQura with [Checkout Sessions](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive), [Payment Links](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment-link.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive) and [PaymentIntents](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment_intents.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive).

## Impact

You can integrate SeQura into your checkout flow to let your customers pay in instalments. SeQura is a single-use payment method that you can’t save for future payments or use for subscriptions, so your customers complete the authorisation flow for each transaction.
