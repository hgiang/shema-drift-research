# Adds proration details for Invoice Items using classic billing mode

## What’s new

Adds [proration_details](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoiceitems/object.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive#invoiceitem_object-proration_details) to proration invoice items for [Subscriptions](https://docs.stripe.com/api/subscriptions/object.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive) using [classic billing mode](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/billing-mode/compare.md).

The [proration_details](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoiceitems/object.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive#invoiceitem_object-proration_details) hash includes [credited_items](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoiceitems/object.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive#invoiceitem_object-proration_details-credited_items), which provides a reference to the corresponding debit for credit proration invoice items.

## Impact

The [proration_details.credited_items](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoiceitems/object.md?api-version=2026-09-30.endive#invoiceitem_object-proration_details-credited_items) property helps you [relate credit prorations to their corresponding debits](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/prorations.md#credit-proration-debit-links), making it easier to track and explain proration adjustments to your customers.
