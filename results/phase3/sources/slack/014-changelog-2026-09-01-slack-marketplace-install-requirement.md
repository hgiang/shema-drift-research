# Updated installation requirement for Slack Marketplace apps

September 1, 2026

As of July 2026, we require that apps submitting to the Slack Marketplace have at least 10 installations on active workspaces, up from the temporary lower threshold. Your app needs to stay at 10 or more installed workspaces throughout the _entire duration of the review_.
